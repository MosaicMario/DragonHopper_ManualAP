# Manual AP for Dragon Hopper
Dragon Hopper is a top down platform adventure game released on the Virtual Boy - Nintendo Classics App for the Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2. Take on the role of Dorin, a dragon prince who has to save his girlfriend Diana from the clutches of the evil prime minister Lexer. Jump and fight your way through 7 different worlds, gaining new powers from transformations and magic spells along the way.

## How does this work?
This manual uses the "Secret Options" menu, which includes a complete stage select, as well as an "all magic" toggle. This menu can be accessed with the following button sequence on the title screen:
> L-Right, R-Left, L-Up, R-Down, L-Left, R-Right, L-Down, R-Up, Select, Start

You will use the stage select options, as well as the all magic toggle as described in the rules section below.

## Goal
You goal is to defeat both Lexer and King Hades in order to rescue the King, Queen and your love Diana, saving Celestia in the process.

In order to fight Lexer you need to meet **one** of the 3 requirements set in your player options:
- Collect the specified number of Star Medals
- Defeat the specified number of bosses
- Release the specified number of spirits

In order to fight King Hades you need to have saved the King and Queen, as well as having found the Star Crystal to make him vulnerable.

You will also need to find the proper Stage Unlock item for both bosses:
- Unlock Stage 6 for both if Stagesanity is disabled
- Unlock Stage 6-6 for Lexer and Unlock Stage 6-8 for King Hades if Stagesanity is enabled

## Items
- Stage Unlocks, including the 2 Bonus Stages
- Star Medals (MacGuffins)
- The Star Crytal
- Transformations
- Small and Big Keys
- Seline and Magic Cards

## Locations
- Star Medals, 90 in Stages and 20 in Bonus Stages
- Small Key and Big Key pickups
- Obtaining transformations
- Goaling a stage
- 11 Magic Cards at the Trading Post for 10 Star Medals each
- 6 Bonus Game Clerk locations, one for each stage a Trading Post can be found in
- Other story related locations

## Da rules
1. You may only access and play unlocked stages.
2. You may only use transformations that have been unlocked.
3. You may only use keys in stages you have received the appropriate keys for.
4. Enable the all magic toggle in the secret options to obtain all magic cards.
5. You may only use a magic card if you have the card itself and Seline unlocked.
6. You may use a "One Time Unrestricted Magic Use" item to cast any magic spell, ignoring rule 5. Using one permanently removes it from your inventory, so use them wisely.
7. Purchase Magic Card checks require you to find a Trading Post in either Stage 1-4, 2-4, 3-4, 4-4, 5-4 or 6-5, and talking to the shop keeper to claim all pending checks. You do not need to purchase anything in-game.
8. Bonus Game Clerk checks require you to find the Trading Post in the corresponding stage and talking to the bonus game clerk. However, you do not get access to the Bonus Stages this way.
9. Bonus Stage Medal checks require you to obtain the number of medals in one attempt and are not meant to be cumulative.
10. When death link is enabled you will send a death link when running out of hearts. Receiving a death link forces you restart a stage by ending your game and entering the stage again.

## Stagesanity
Stagesanity is a toggleable setting in your player options.
If Stagesanity is disabled all Substages of a stage become unlocked when the stage unlock item is collected, eg. "Unlock Stage 0" unlocks Stages 0-1, 0-2 and 0-3.
If Stagesanity is enabled all Substages of a stage bocome their own unlock item, eg. "Unlock Stage 1-1" unlocks Stage 1-1 and "Unlock Stage 2-2" unlocks Stage 2-2.

## Notes and considerations
- Due to the linear nature of this game it is not possible to use transformations that are unlocked later in the game. This limitation also necessitates having both the Big Key and the matching transformation items in Stages 2-3, 3-3 and 4-3 in order to progress further in those.
- Technically, the two Star Medals in the pits of Stages 4-2 and 4-4 requiring the Fire Dragon can be obtained without it by ending the game and re-entering the level. However, this felt very cheap to me, so I made thawing the jump pads to get out of the pits a requirement. Alternatively, you can use Seline + Flashback Card to get out of those pits too.
- The Big Key in Stage 3-3 can be obtained without the Earth Dragon by jumping off the edge of the floor above the platform with the teleporter and flapping towards it. This jump is a little tricky, but doable. It becomes easier with the Speed Card.
